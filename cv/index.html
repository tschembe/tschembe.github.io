Projects

  • 2026 - 2026
    Plantwide Control of an Ethylbenzene Plant in AVEVA Process Simulation

    Master project (10 ECTS), TH Köln

    • Control concept for a plant with 2 reactors, 2 columns and 2 recycles (Buckley/Luyben): degree-of-freedom analysis (16 valves, 9 specifications) and P&IDs to DIN EN 62424.
    • Full plant with custom kinetics and 16 PID controllers in AVEVA; audited the simulation files and found the cause of an oversized recycle stream.
    • Independent plant model in Python (within 1.3 % of the literature). The recycle controller limits the snowball effect at +20 % throughput to +29 % instead of +91 %.
  • 2025 - 2026
    System Identification of a Slug Flow Crystallizer with Machine Learning

    Project study TH Köln / elective project TU Dortmund

    • Trained NARX networks (MATLAB, PyTorch) on about 370,000 time steps; derived the input window from the residence time and confirmed it with impulse-response analysis.
    • Cut the recursive prediction error of crystal size d50 from 44 µm to 0.41 µm (R² = 0.999). PCA made the model 9× smaller and 4× more accurate.
    • Sensitivity analysis: the cooling-jacket inlet temperature is the strongest input (+4.3 K → −14.7 µm), a basis for model-predictive control.
  • 2025 - 2026
    Electrodeposition from Deep Eutectic Solvents

    Process Engineering Conference, TH Köln, winter term 2025/26 (Best Poster Award)

    • Literature review of about 30 publications from a process engineering view; extended abstract, A0 poster and conference paper as sole author.
    • Screening model in Python (mass transfer, Butler–Volmer kinetics, ohmic losses, Wagner number), validated against literature data within 4 %.
    • Result: mass transfer is the limit. A DES bath needs about 19× the tank area of an aqueous bath; water and temperature together reduce this to 2.5×.
  • 2025 - 2025
    Design of a CSTR for Styrene Polymerisation

    Module Polymer Engineering and Recycling, TH Köln

    • Solved mass and energy balances with a kinetic model (incl. gel effect) in MATLAB; sized the reactor and heat exchangers; designed a two-stage cooling system; made BFD, PFD and pre-HAZOP.
    • Re-checked the design in Python: model validated against literature data (within 0.22 %-points); corrected reactor volume and operating pressure (0.81 bar from vapour-liquid equilibrium).
    • Simulated a loss of cooling: 43 to 65 min until 200 °C, as input for the safety review.
  • 2024 - 2025
    Scale-up and Cost Estimation of a Fluidised-Bed Reactor for Vinyl Acetate

    Module Scale-up and Cost Estimation, TH Köln

    • Mass and energy balances in CHEMCAD; scaled the reactor 200× (10 kg/h → 2 t/h) with dimensionless numbers; dynamic reactor model in MATLAB.
    • AACE class 4 cost estimate (Hand factors, CEPCI): 3.0 M€ investment and about 1,940 €/t production cost.
    • Confirmed the scale-up with a two-phase model (32.0 % conversion at both scales). A van Heerden analysis found an unstable operating point; PI control holds it within ±1.1 K.
