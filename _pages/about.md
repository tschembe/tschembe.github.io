I am a master's student in Process Engineering at TH Köln. My focus is process design, process simulation and control.

In my projects I have worked along the whole chain: mass and energy balances, reactor design, scale-up and cost estimation, dynamic simulation in AVEVA, CHEMCAD and MATLAB, and data-driven modelling in Python. I like to check a model against literature or measured data before I trust it. I close the balances, and I write down what I did so others can follow it.

Right now I am an intern at the :metabolon institute of TH Köln, where I work on iron membranes for hydrogen separation. Before my master's, I studied chemical engineering in Tehran and worked in quality control in chemical production.

I am looking for my first full-time role as a process engineer, for example in process development, plant engineering or simulation. You can find short summaries of my work on the [projects](/projects/) page and my full background in my [CV](/cv/).
